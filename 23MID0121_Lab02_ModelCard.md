## Intended Use
This model is intended for **educational purposes** to demonstrate the application of Decision Tree algorithms (CART, cost-complexity pruning, hyperparameter tuning via GridSearchCV) for binary medical classification tasks, and to illustrate the trade-off between interpretability (Decision Tree) and predictive performance (Random Forest).

## Out-of-Scope Use
- This model should **not** be used for clinical diagnosis.
- It should **not** replace healthcare professionals.
- It has **not** been externally validated on real hospital data.
- Predictions must **not** be interpreted as medical advice.

## Limitations
- The dataset is relatively small (569 samples).
- External validation on independent/real hospital data was not performed.
- Class distribution (63% malignant-relabeled / benign split as used here) may differ from real clinical populations.
- Performance may decrease on unseen hospital data or different patient populations.
- Decision Trees are sensitive to the specific training sample, although cost-complexity pruning reduces overfitting.
- The higher-performing Random Forest is less interpretable than the single pruned Decision Tree that was ultimately selected.

## Fairness & Ethical Considerations
- The dataset may not represent all patient populations.
- Model predictions could vary across demographic groups not captured in this dataset (e.g., age, ethnicity — not present as features here).
- Fairness metrics were not explicitly evaluated.
- Human oversight by a qualified clinician is required before any healthcare decision is made using this model's output.
